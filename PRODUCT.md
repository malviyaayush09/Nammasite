## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are local Bangalore business owners and operators who need a professional website quickly: salons, gyms, restaurants, clinics, florists, boutiques, preschools, interior designers, tattoo studios, coaching centres, and similar neighborhood businesses.

## Product Purpose

NammaSite designs and builds custom websites for local businesses. The key customer promise is that a business can see a live website sample before paying, then approve and pay only if the result earns trust.

## Positioning

The product's distinct mechanism is the risk reversal: NammaSite builds first, sends a real working link, and asks for payment only after approval. Current public copy states a 48-hour launch promise, no advance, WhatsApp-led support, and flat pricing.

## Operating Context

Customers are expected to start through WhatsApp or a 30-minute Calendly call. The existing flow asks for a business name first, then later collects photos, timings, services, location, and other details once the sample is approved.

## Product Principles

- Show proof before asking for commitment.
- Keep the offer concrete: price, time, ownership, and next action should be visible without hunting.
- Make the experience local and direct rather than corporate or agency-like.
- Explain the real buying questions competitors usually answer: what is included, who the plan fits, what information is needed, how launch is checked, and what support exists after launch.
- Preserve real project evidence and avoid ornamental proof.
- Keep the first action low-friction: business name, WhatsApp, or call.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should remain usable at 320px and above, with semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, accessible dialogs and accordions, useful validation messages, and reduced-motion support.
